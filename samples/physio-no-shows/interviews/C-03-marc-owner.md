Interviewer: How do people book with you?

Marc: Phone and a free online booking page I set up. About half book online now, mostly the under-40s.

Interviewer: How often do patients not show up?

Marc: Maybe once or twice a week. For a solo practice that hurts, because when someone doesn't come I'm not earning anything for that hour but I still pay the room rent.

Interviewer: What do you do about it?

Marc: My online booking page sends an automatic email reminder. It helped a bit, but emails get ignored. I think a text message would work better.

Interviewer: Would you switch your main practice software for better no-show handling?

Marc: No. I just moved to my current system last year and it was painful. I lost a weekend to exporting and re-importing patient files. Not doing that again for a few years.
