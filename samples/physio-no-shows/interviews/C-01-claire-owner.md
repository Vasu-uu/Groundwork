**Interviewer:** What does a normal week look like at the clinic?

**Claire:** Three therapists, about 120 appointments a week. I still treat patients myself in the mornings and do admin in the afternoon.

**Interviewer:** What's the most frustrating part of running the schedule?

**Claire:** No-shows, without question. We get six to eight no-shows a week, and each one is an empty 45-minute slot I can't fill at the last minute. That's roughly 400 euros a week just gone.

**Interviewer:** How do you try to prevent them today?

**Claire:** Our receptionist phones everyone the day before. It takes her most of the afternoon, and half the time she gets voicemail.

**Interviewer:** Do you have a waiting list?

**Claire:** On paper, yes. In reality, by the time we find someone on the list who can come in, the slot has passed.

**Interviewer:** What about online booking?

**Claire:** Our older patients would never use it. They phone, they want to talk to a person. Maybe the younger sports patients would like it.

**Interviewer:** Would you consider a different practice software if it solved no-shows?

**Claire:** Switching software is my nightmare. We have seven years of patient notes in our current system. I would only consider something that plugs into what we already use.
