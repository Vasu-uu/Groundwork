Interviewer: Is food waste a problem for you?
Hana: Honestly, not much. Our menu is short on purpose, ten dishes, and we reuse everything. Yesterday's bread becomes today's French toast. I'd say waste is under 3 percent.
Interviewer: How do you order?
Hana: Daily, from a local bakery and a market wholesaler. They don't have minimums for regular customers, so I can order exactly what I need.
Interviewer: Do you track inventory?
Hana: On a whiteboard in the kitchen. An app would be one more thing to update, and my staff wouldn't keep it up.
Interviewer: What is your biggest operational headache?
Hana: Staffing. Finding people for weekend shifts is much harder than anything to do with food.
