**Interviewer:** How do you decide how much to order each week?

**Paolo:** Gut feeling and last week's numbers on a notepad. I order on Sunday night for the whole week.

**Interviewer:** How much food ends up thrown away?

**Paolo:** Too much. Fish and herbs especially. I'd guess we throw away 10 to 15 percent of what we buy in a slow week. When it rains, nobody comes, and I've got a fridge full of sea bass.

**Interviewer:** Why not order smaller amounts more often?

**Paolo:** My fish supplier has a minimum order of 150 euros per delivery. If I only need half of that, I still have to buy 150 euros, so I buy extra and hope.

**Interviewer:** Would you use an app to log your stock every day?

**Paolo:** Every day? No. After a 12-hour service the last thing I want is to count tomatoes on my phone. If it worked from my supplier invoices automatically, maybe.
