Interviewer: Walk me through your ordering.

Kenji: Each location orders separately twice a week. The managers guess, really. We over-order because running out of broth on a Friday night is a disaster.

Interviewer: How much does over-ordering cost you?

Kenji: Last quarter we threw away about 2,400 euros of vegetables and pork across both sites. My accountant flagged it.

Interviewer: Do minimum orders play a role?

Kenji: Yes, our meat supplier only delivers if we order at least 20 kilos. For the smaller location that's more than a week's worth, so some of it goes to waste.

Interviewer: Would your managers use a daily inventory app?

Kenji: I'd love them to, but they won't. We tried a stock-taking app last year and they stopped after two weeks. It took 30 minutes every night.
