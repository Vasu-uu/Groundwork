**Interviewer:** How do you usually work with clients?

**Ade:** Mostly YouTube creators. Fast projects, lots of small invoices, like 300 to 800 euros each.

**Interviewer:** How does getting paid go?

**Ade:** It's chaos. Creators are not businesses, a lot of them forget invoices exist. I have to chase almost every single one, and some I just give up on because chasing a 300 euro invoice for weeks isn't worth the stress.

**Interviewer:** You give up on invoices?

**Ade:** Yeah. Last year I wrote off about 1,500 euros. I didn't want to be the annoying editor who keeps asking for money, because creators talk to each other and I need referrals.

**Interviewer:** How does that affect your month-to-month?

**Ade:** I can't plan anything. Some months I get four payments in a week and some months nothing for three weeks. I've had my card declined at the supermarket while clients owed me 2,000 euros.

**Interviewer:** If a tool chased payments for you, politely and automatically, would you pay for it?

**Ade:** Yes, honestly, I would pay for that today. If it saved me even one write-off a year it pays for itself.

**Interviewer:** And the VAT and tax side?

**Ade:** I'm confused about whether I need to charge VAT to creators in other countries. I just don't, and I hope it's fine.

**Interviewer:** How long does it take you to make an invoice?

**Ade:** Making the invoice is fast, I use a template. Remembering to send it is the hard part, sometimes I forget for a week.
