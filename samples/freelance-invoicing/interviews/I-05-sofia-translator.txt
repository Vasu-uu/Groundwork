Interviewer: How do you get paid by your clients?
Sofia: Mostly agencies. They have a monthly payment run, so I send all my invoices before the 25th and get paid on the 10th of the next month. It's very regular.
Interviewer: So late payments aren't an issue for you?
Sofia: Not really. Maybe once a year an agency is late and one email fixes it. I know that's not normal for other freelancers, but translation agencies are quite organised.
Interviewer: What's the hardest part of the money side for you, then?
Sofia: Keeping track of everything across currencies. I work with agencies in three countries, so I have euros, pounds and Swiss francs, and my spreadsheet is a nightmare at tax time.
Interviewer: Tell me more about tax time.
Sofia: VAT rules for clients outside the country confuse me every single year. I'm never sure if I filled in the reverse-charge box correctly, so I pay an accountant 400 euros just for that.
Interviewer: How long does it take you to create an invoice?
Sofia: Very little. The agencies often generate the purchase order and I just copy the numbers into my template.
Interviewer: Would you pay for automatic payment reminders?
Sofia: No, I wouldn't use them. My clients pay on time.
