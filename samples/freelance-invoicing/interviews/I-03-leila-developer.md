Q: What does your invoicing process look like?

A: I invoice at milestones. I use a free invoicing tool, the invoice itself is quick. The pain starts after.

Q: What do you mean by "after"?

A: Startups are the worst payers. They're lovely people, but when their runway is short, the freelancer is the first bill they delay. I've had to chase one client for six weeks and I ended up texting the CEO directly, which felt awful.

Q: How do you keep track of outstanding payments?

A: Honestly, my email inbox and my memory. I search for "invoice" and check what has no reply. I've definitely forgotten to follow up on an invoice before and only noticed months later.

Q: How much is usually outstanding?

A: Right now about 9,000 euros across three clients. That's two months of my living costs just sitting there.

Q: Would automated reminders help?

A: My tool already sends reminders and I wouldn't pay extra for that. Reminders are free everywhere now. What I'd pay for is something that tells me which client is likely to pay late before I sign with them.
