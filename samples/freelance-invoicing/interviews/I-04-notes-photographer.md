# Notes from call with Jonas, event photographer

- Shoots weddings and corporate events, around 40 jobs a year.
- Takes a 30 percent deposit for weddings, so private clients are rarely a problem.
- Corporate clients are the problem: purchase orders, supplier registration forms, invoices bounced back for missing PO numbers.
- Said chasing corporate invoices is "a second unpaid job" and he puts it off for weeks because it feels awkward.
- Biggest late payment this year was 3,200 euros, paid after almost four months.
- Uses a spreadsheet plus calendar reminders to follow up.
- Invoices themselves take him a few minutes, he did not see that as a problem.
- Was lukewarm on paying for a reminder tool: "the reminder isn't the problem, the PO process is."
